### 🔴 Los 5 errores que se repiten
1. ***all the day*** → **all day** (3 veces)
2. ***too many*** → **a lot of** (2 veces; *too many* = demasiados, negativo)
3. ***since*** + pasado → **present perfect**: *I've been working since February* · *it's been hot lately*
4. ***used to*** = hábito pasado → hoy es **usually**
5. **Preposiciones:** *in March · at a company · on public transport · at night · listen to*
